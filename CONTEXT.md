# Application Knowledge

This context describes reusable knowledge about a candidate that supports truthful job applications and interview preparation.

## Language

**Candidate**:
Proposed reusable knowledge awaiting an explicit decision. A candidate is pending review, deferred, rejected, or promoted; there is no separate confirmed-but-unpromoted state.
_Avoid_: Unverified fact, draft fact

**Confirmed**:
A knowledge status meaning that the person described has reviewed the candidate and attests that it is accurate enough to reuse. Independent corroboration is not required.
_Avoid_: Verified

**Corroborated**:
A knowledge status meaning that a source independent of the person's direct attestation supports the knowledge.
_Avoid_: Confirmed, verified

**Profile Knowledge**:
A confirmed claim or story that has been promoted for reuse across applications and interview preparation.
_Avoid_: Master-profile fact, verified evidence

**Claim**:
One independently reusable assertion about the person, identified stably so it can be referenced across applications and stories.
_Avoid_: Bullet, evidence

**Story**:
A reusable account of an episode, identified stably and structured around its situation, stakes, decision, alternatives, contribution, result, verification, and lesson. A story references the claims it demonstrates.
_Avoid_: Claim, interview answer

**Story Gap**:
An explicitly missing element of a promoted story. A story may be promoted when its core episode and the person's contribution are confirmed; gaps remain unanswered rather than being inferred.
_Avoid_: Assumption, placeholder fact

**CV Bullet**:
An application-specific representation generated from Profile Knowledge. It is not itself Profile Knowledge and does not become reusable merely because it appears in a CV.
_Avoid_: Claim, profile fact

**Promotion**:
The explicit, atomic decision to confirm a candidate and add it to Profile Knowledge while preserving its provenance. Promotion requires choosing whether to create new knowledge, revise existing knowledge, or link another source to existing knowledge; suggested matches are never merged automatically.
_Avoid_: Approval, automatic learning

**Revision**:
A traceable correction or refinement of Profile Knowledge that preserves its stable identity, earlier wording, reason, source, and confirmation date.
_Avoid_: Overwrite, replacement

**Retired**:
A status for Profile Knowledge that must not be selected for new material but remains available to explain historical applications and provenance.
_Avoid_: Deleted, rejected

**Application Note**:
Accurate information retained for one application but not intended for reuse as Profile Knowledge.
_Avoid_: Candidate, profile fact

**Source**:
Material or testimony from which a candidate was derived. The person's direct answer is a valid source; other artifacts may independently corroborate it.
_Avoid_: Proof
